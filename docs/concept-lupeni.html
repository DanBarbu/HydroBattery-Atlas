Why this site

Lupeni was the largest and longest-lived mine in the Jiu Valley (operating since the 1880s) and is one of the basin's two storage anchors: ~700 m deep workings under a ~640 m ASL valley floor give ~650 m of usable head over ~1.5 Mm³ of accessible void — roughly 2.3 GWh.

It also holds the one option no other Romanian mine offers: the hybrid head play. The valley floor sits directly beneath the Straja/Vâlcan ridges (1,400–1,870 m ASL, with existing road access via the Straja resort). An upper reservoir on the uninhabited slope above the town, paired with the mine as the lower reservoir, stacks surface relief on top of mine depth for a total head potentially exceeding 1,000 m — turbine-class head that transforms the economics of every stored cubic metre.

The closing window

Under the closure program, galleries are sealed with concrete dams one by one and shafts are ultimately backfilled. Backfilled shafts are effectively unrecoverable for PHES at any reasonable cost. A formal request to SNIMVJ to pause shaft backfilling pending a storage feasibility review is the single highest-value action available — it costs nothing and preserves a ~2.3 GWh option (and the only >1,000 m head option in the country).

Risks and engineering burden

  • Methane — gassy mine, basin-standard inertisation/ventilation precondition;
  • Acid mine water — treatment or isolation of the cycling volume (flagged in the Atlas record);
  • Weak rock — coal measures; machine-hall siting needs geotechnical screening;
  • Hybrid variant — penstock/shaft route from the Straja slope to the workings crosses steep terrain; the upper reservoir must avoid the ski domain and stay on uninhabited slope per the Atlas siting rule.

What the SNIMVJ closure dossier must confirm

  • Closure progress: which levels are already sealed, which shafts remain open, the backfilling schedule;
  • Gallery/chamber volumes by level; current mine-water level;
  • Residual methane regime;
  • Surface land status above the town for the hybrid upper reservoir.
